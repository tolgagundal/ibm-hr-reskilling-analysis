# People Analytics: Understanding Employee Attrition and Improving Employee Experience

This project demonstrates an end-to-end People Analytics workflow using Google BigQuery, Python, and Tableau to explore the factors associated with employee attrition and translate analytical findings into actionable HR recommendations.

## Business Question
How can HR or People Teams reduce employee attrition and improve employee experience using data?

## Why This Matters
Employee attrition is one of the most expensive workforce challenges. Beyond recruitment costs, organizations lose knowledge, productivity, and employee engagement. People Analytics helps HR teams identify patterns associated with attrition and make more informed workforce decisions.

## Dataset
[IBM HR Employee Attrition Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset)
- 1,470 employees
- 35 HR-related features including age, department, job role, monthly income, training frequency, job satisfaction, work-life balance, tenure, and attrition
- Cross-sectional workforce snapshot

## SQL Analysis (What I built)

1. Workforce Overview
   - Employee demographics
   - Department and job role distribution

2. Attrition Analysis
   - Attrition by department
   - Attrition by job role
   - Attrition by overtime
   - Attrition by income level

3. Learning & Development
   - Training frequency by department
   - Training frequency by attrition status

4. Employee Experience
   - Job satisfaction
   - Environment satisfaction
   - Work-life balance
   - Relationship satisfaction

## Python Analysis (What I calculated)

1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
2. Correlation analysis between HR factors and attrition
3. Feature importance using Logistic Regression coefficients
4. Predictive modeling to estimate employee attrition risk
5. Business recommendations based on analytical insights

## Key Metrics

- Attrition Rate by Department and Job Role
- Attrition Rate by Overtime Status
- Average Job Satisfaction and Work-Life Balance
- Training Frequency by Attrition Status
